## Course Overview

This course examines how organisations design, manage, and deliver information services to create value. It focuses on the integration of technology, business processes, and organisational strategy.

Students will:
- Understand service-oriented architectures and IT service models
- Analyse digital platforms and service ecosystems
- Evaluate how IT services contribute to business value
- Apply frameworks for managing information services
- Study real-world cases of service design and delivery

## Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of information systems
- Interest in IT management and digital services

## Assessment

- Assignments on service design and analysis
- Case study project
- Participation

## Teaching Approach

The course combines:
- Lectures on service concepts and frameworks
- Case-based learning
- Practical assignments
- Discussions on digital transformation and service innovation
