## Course Overview

This course introduces the concepts and technologies behind Industry 4.0 and examines how digital transformation reshapes industrial processes and organisations.

Students will:
- Understand key technologies such as IoT, cyber-physical systems, and data analytics
- Analyse the impact of digital transformation on industrial processes
- Explore human-centered design in technology-driven environments
- Evaluate risks and cybersecurity challenges in Industry 4.0
- Study real-world industrial applications

## Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of information systems or engineering concepts
- Interest in digital transformation and industrial innovation

## Assessment

- Assignments on Industry 4.0 concepts and applications
- Case study project
- Participation

## Teaching Approach

The course combines:
- Conceptual lectures on Industry 4.0 technologies
- Case-based learning from industrial applications
- Practical assignments
- Discussions on human-centered digital transformation
