## Course Overview

This course focuses on the design and management of security within large organisations. It combines technical security concepts with organisational and governance perspectives.

Students will:
- Understand enterprise security architecture and frameworks
- Analyse risks at organisational and system levels
- Design security strategies aligned with business goals
- Evaluate governance and compliance requirements
- Study real-world enterprise security cases

## Prerequisites

- Basic knowledge of cybersecurity concepts
- Familiarity with information systems

## Assessment

- Assignments on enterprise security design
- Case study analysis
- Participation

## Teaching Approach

The course combines:
- Lectures on enterprise security frameworks
- Case-based learning from real incidents
- Practical assignments on security design
- Discussions on governance and policy
