Abhishta Abhishta is an associate professor within the Industrial Engineering and Business Information Systems group at the University of Twente, the Netherlands. He holds a BE in Industrial Engineering from Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (2013), an MBA in Operations and IT from Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (2015), and a PhD in Security Economics from the University of Twente (2019). His research focuses on empirically measuring the impact of cyber attacks, using data-driven economic impact assessment techniques to support well-informed security investments. His research interests include security economics, network measurements, and cyber risk management.
